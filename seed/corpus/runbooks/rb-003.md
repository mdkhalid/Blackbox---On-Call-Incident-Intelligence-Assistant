## Symptom
payment-gateway reports ERR_POOL_EXHAUSTED; p99 latency spiking; requests pile up.

## Diagnosis
1. Check connection pool metrics for payment-gateway.
2. Verify upstream dependencies: none.
3. Look for ERR_POOL_EXHAUSTED in logs.
4. Confirm no recent deploy at the time of incident.

## Mitigation
- Restart payment-gateway pods.
- Scale up connection pool.
- Enable circuit breaker to upstream.

## Escalation
Escalate to platform team if not resolved in 30 minutes. Page service owner.
